    Why it works

    pre-train: maximize Σ log p(xᵢ | x₍<ᵢ₎) fine-tune: minimize CE( head(h_last), label )

    Next-token pre-training forces the final hidden state to summarize the sentence; the classifier then only has to read that summary — so it learns from far fewer labels.
