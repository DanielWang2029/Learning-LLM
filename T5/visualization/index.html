One framework: text in, text out

A task token selects the behaviour; the architecture never changes. Below are real examples from the trained model.

Pre-training objective: span corruption

Random contiguous spans of the input are dropped and replaced by sentinel tokens (<X0>,<X1>…). The decoder must regenerate the missing spans, each announced by its sentinel.
