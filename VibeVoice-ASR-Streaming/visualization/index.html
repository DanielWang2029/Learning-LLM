VibeVoice-ASR-Streaming

One of the first LLM-based approaches to streaming speaker-attributed ASR. It interleaves fixed-size audio chunks, a small lookahead, and previously generated text in a single autoregressive context — emitting “who said what” as speech arrives, with no separate diarization stage.

Tu et al., Microsoft, 2026 arXiv:2609.02812 Interleaved speech-text Lookahead + chunk-end token

This page reproduces the paper's mechanism at small scale on CPU. Data below is baked in from a real run of demo/run_demo.py (a tiny interleaved speech-text Transformer trained on two-speaker synthetic audio). Serve over http to load live data.

The interleaved streaming sequence

§3.1: the model consumes one shared autoregressive stream [X₁, Y₁, X₂, Y₂, …]. Each speech chunk Xₖ is its audio frames plus a fixed lookahead (future evidence near the boundary); each Yₖ is the speaker-attributed text, terminated by a <chunk_end> token that hands control back to the audio stream.

audio frame (onset/peak) lookahead frame (future) generated text <chunk_end>

Speaker labels are assigned by order of first appearance and reused via the retained history — a label introduced in an early chunk conditions every later chunk. That history is what fixes speaker identity with no diarization module.

Why the lookahead matters

The last phone of each chunk reveals its identity only in its peak frame, which lands in the lookahead. Removing the lookahead cripples transcription at chunk boundaries — exactly the “limited future acoustic evidence near chunk boundaries” the paper adds (Eq. 2, L=4 frames ≈ 0.5 s).

Accuracy: with vs without lookahead (40 test conversations)

The factorization

p(Yₖ | X<ₖ, X̃ₖ, Y<ₖ) = Π_j p(y_{k,j} | X<ₖ, X̃ₖ, Y<ₖ, y_{k,<j}) X̃ₖ = chunk Xₖ + L lookahead frames Yₖ ends with <chunk_end> (model decides when) lookahead: T = L · 3200 / 24000 ≈ 0.5 s (L=4)
  • WHO is speaking is audible from a chunk's onset frames.
  • WHAT was said at the boundary needs the lookahead's peak frame.
  • History Y<ₖ keeps speaker labels consistent across the whole call.
