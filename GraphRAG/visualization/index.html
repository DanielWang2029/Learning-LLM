GraphRAG: From Local to Global

Vanilla RAG retrieves a few similar chunks — perfect for local facts, useless for global sensemaking. GraphRAG builds a knowledge graph, detects communities, summarizes each, and answers a global query by map-reduce over those summaries. Every figure below is real output from this folder's demo.
