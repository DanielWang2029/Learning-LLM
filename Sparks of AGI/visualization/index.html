Sparks of AGI · Capability Profile vs. Scale

“Sparks of Artificial General Intelligence” is an analysis paper — it introduces no new model or algorithm. Its contribution is a methodology: probe a model with a broad battery of self-contained tasks and report which capabilities are present. This page reproduces that method with a battery of six transparent probes and a family of tiny GPTs, showing the capability profile fill in as scale grows.

Bubeck et al., 2023 arXiv 2303.12712 capability probing analysis, not an algorithm
Honesty note. There is no “Sparks” algorithm to reproduce — the paper studies GPT-4's behavior. What is reproducible is its experimental method: a battery of probes turned into a pass/fail capability profile, tracked across model scale. Everything below is real: tiny GPTs actually trained on each probe, on CPU, in ~30 seconds.
