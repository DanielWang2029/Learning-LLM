Gated DeltaNet-2

    Why decouple? One scalar was doing two jobs

    DeltaNet / Gated DeltaNet / KDA use a single scalar gate β_t for the active edit. But erasing is a key-side decision (which coordinates of the old read to remove) and writing is a value-side decision (which coordinates of the new value to commit). These need not agree, so Gated DeltaNet-2 gives each its own channel-wise gate.

    Prior work: tied scalar gate

    DeltaNet: S = (I − β k kᵀ) S + β k vᵀ Gated DeltaNet: S = α (I − β k kᵀ) S + β k vᵀ KDA: S = (I − β k kᵀ) D S + β k vᵀ └────── one β for erase AND write ──────┘

    A single β ties how much to forget on the key side to how much to store on the value side.

    Gated DeltaNet-2: two gates

    e = b ⊙ k b ∈ [0,1]^d_k (erase, key axis) z = w ⊙ v w ∈ [0,1]^d_v (write, value axis) S = (I − k (b⊙k)ᵀ) D S + k (w⊙v)ᵀ

    Recovers KDA when b = w = β·1, and Gated DeltaNet when the decay is scalar too — strictly more expressive.
