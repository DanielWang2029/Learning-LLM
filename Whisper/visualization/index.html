Whisper

Robust speech recognition as one clean encoder-decoder Transformer. Log-Mel audio → a conv stem that downsamples → a Transformer encoder → a Transformer decoder that emits text tokens via cross-attention. Reads a fixed 30 s chunk and steers every task with multitask tokens. Click a stage to explore.

Radford et al., 2022 arXiv:2212.04356 Encoder–Decoder 80-bin log-Mel

The model, end to end

Audio flows up the encoder; text is generated down the decoder, which attends to the encoder output via cross-attention (Section 2.2).
