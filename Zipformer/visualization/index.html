Zipformer

A U-Net-like encoder whose middle stacks run at a lower frame rate, plus BiasNorm (a LayerNorm that keeps length information) and learned Bypass connections — faster and better than Conformer.

    BiasNorm — a LayerNorm that remembers length

    LayerNorm removes the vector length entirely, so trained Conformers sometimes stash a huge constant in one channel to defeat it. BiasNorm keeps length via a learnable bias and uses a strictly-positive scale (Zipformer §3.3).

    LayerNorm (baseline)

    LayerNorm(x) = (x − E[x]) / √(Var[x]+ε) ⊙ γ + β · subtracts the mean · scale γ can oscillate around 0 (gradient sign flips) · length information is fully removed

    BiasNorm (Zipformer §3.3)

    BiasNorm(x) = x / RMS[x − b] · exp(γ) · no mean subtraction (cheaper) · learnable bias b retains length information · exp(γ) > 0 avoids gradient-sign oscillation
