    Foundation training: the M²-OPD pipeline (§3)

    An off-policy cold start, then on-policy distillation that scores the student on its own trajectories. The reproduction trains all three boxes.

    Multi-Teacher OPD

    Text Teacher qT — reads clean text xt, scores reasoning/tools Audio Reference qref — frozen; supplies audio-only behavior
    y ~ π_θ(· | x_a) (student samples its OWN trajectory) p_θ^(j) = π_θ(· | x_a, y_<j) (student, on its own prefix) q_T^(j) = q_T(· | x_t, y_<j) (Text Teacher scores that prefix) q_ref^(j) = q_ref(· | x_a, y_<j) (frozen Audio Reference) → update p_θ toward q_T (text-representable) and q_ref (audio-only)

    Reproduction results (baked from the demo)

    [A] On-policy reliability (exposure bias)

    Coverage of the transition function over the whole state space — including states the student only reaches by mistake. Off-policy SFT sees only gold states; on-policy OPD covers the rest.

    [B] Audio-only attribute retention

    Accuracy on the audio-only STYLE the Text Teacher cannot see. Text-only OPD forgets it; the frozen Audio Reference in M²-OPD preserves it.
