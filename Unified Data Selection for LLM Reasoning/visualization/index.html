High-Entropy Sum (HES)

A training-free metric for selecting high-quality LLM reasoning data: score a chain-of-thought by summing the entropy of only its top-p% highest-entropy "critical" tokens. Averaging dilutes the signal; the summation over the critical forks does not.

Li et al., 2026arXiv:2605.22389 §3HESSFT · RFT · RL
HES_relative(y) = Σ{ t : rank(Hₜ) ∈ top p% } Hₜ (sum entropy of the top-p% critical tokens)

2. HES ranks quality; averaging fails

AUC = P[score(high-quality) > score(low-quality)]. Only the summation metrics (HES, ES) separate quality; the averaging metrics (AvgE, AvgHE) do not — the paper's Fig. 1 finding.

Mean HES by quality tier

HES cleanly orders a long, detailed derivation (HQ) above a short answer or a filler-padded one.

3. HES-selected SFT data matches the full set — with 1/5 of the data

Train a fresh student on each subset (equal steps), measure held-out exact-match. The top-20% by HES matches the full dataset and far exceeds a random 20%; the lowest-HES data is worst.
