LIMO — Less Is More for Reasoning

A handful of high-quality step-by-step reasoning demonstrations can teach a model to reason better than a much larger pile of low-quality ones. Below, the same tiny model is fine-tuned two ways at an equal training budget — the numbers are real, captured from this folder's demo.

Ye et al., 2025 arXiv:2502.03387 Data quality > quantity SFT for reasoning

Same task, two demonstration qualities

The task is chained single-digit addition modulo 10. Both training sets give the correct answer; they differ only in whether the reasoning is shown. The step-by-step version teaches a reusable single-step skill — which is why so few examples suffice.

HIGH QUALITY · curated · few

Every intermediate running-sum step is written out.

reusable skill: (a + b) mod 10 → learned once, applied at every step
LOW QUALITY · bulk · many

Straight to the answer — no reasoning demonstrated.

must learn: f(a,b,c,d) = (a+b+c+d) mod 10 → hard to generalize from answers alone
