What this isolates—and what it does not

This small experiment isolates the TIR contract documented in §3.1.2: emit a program, execute it, consume the observation. The hand-coded direct and CoT controls deliberately model arithmetic errors, so these values are not Qwen2.5-Math benchmark claims.
