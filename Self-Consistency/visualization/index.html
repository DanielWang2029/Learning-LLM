Self-Consistency

Don't trust one chain of thought — sample many reasoning paths and keep the answer they most agree on. Diverse correct paths reinforce each other; idiosyncratic mistakes get out-voted.

Wang et al., 2022 arXiv:2203.11171 Builds on Chain-of-Thought Marginalize by majority vote

Sample diverse paths → marginalize by majority vote

A question can be reasoned about many ways. Greedy decoding follows only the single most-probable path — which on "trap" problems is a tempting shortcut that's wrong. Sampling explores the many correct paths, and their majority answer wins.

The idea, formally

Greedy chain of thought (baseline)

Decode the single most-probable reasoning path, take its answer.

answer = decode_greedy( reasoning | question ) argmax over PATHS — one shot
  • Commits to one path; if that path is a confident mistake, it's simply wrong.

Self-consistency

Sample many paths, then take the answer most of them reach.

rᵢ, aᵢ ~ p(reasoning, answer | question) i = 1..m answer* = mode( a₁, a₂, …, a_m ) ≈ argmax over ANSWERS (marginalizing paths)
  • Temperature spreads probability across diverse correct paths.
  • The most-probable answer ≠ the most-probable single path.
