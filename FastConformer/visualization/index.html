Fast Conformer

Replace Conformer's 4x convolutional sub-sampling with 8x depthwise-separable sub-sampling. Half the tokens, ~4x cheaper self-attention, and a cheaper front end — with no loss in accuracy.

    Depthwise-separable sub-sampling, unpacked

    A dense convolution mixes across space and channels at once. Depthwise-separable splits that into a cheap per-channel spatial filter plus a 1×1 channel mixer — far fewer multiply-adds (FastConformer §2.1).

    Baseline: 4x regular conv Conformer

    2 × Conv1d(stride 2) frame rate 10 ms → 40 ms cost per layer ≈ T · Cin · Cout · K (dense) tokens out: T / 4

    Fast Conformer: 8x depthwise-separable §2.1

    3 × [ DepthwiseConv1d(K=9, stride 2) → PointwiseConv1d(1×1) ] frame rate 10 ms → 80 ms cost per layer ≈ T·Cin·K + T·Cin·Cout (separable) tokens out: T / 8 ← half of the 4x path
