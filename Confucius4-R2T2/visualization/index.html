Confucius4-R2T2 — Real Real-Time Transcription

A true-streaming ASR decoding paradigm: commit only the Longest Stable Prefix, and never revise text you have already shown. The result is append-only output with no flicker — at configurable chunk sizes from 80 ms to 2 s.

The LSP commit mechanism

Each arriving audio chunk yields a fresh hypothesis for all the audio heard so far. The trailing edge is unstable — the last sound is still ambiguous until it finishes. LSP compares consecutive hypotheses, keeps the prefix on which they agree, holds back a small trailing unfixed window (rollback guard), and permanently commits the rest.

How a token becomes committed

Why the trailing edge is unstable

Our synthetic phones (like real speech) reveal their identity only in their second half. A phone that has just begun is easily mis-recognized, then corrected once its formant arrives — the correction a naive decoder would show as flicker.

Chunk-size sweep — one recognizer, no retraining

Smaller chunks commit sooner (lower latency) but require holding a longer unstable tail; larger chunks are steadier. Across every setting from 80 ms to 2 s, LSP stays append-only (0 revisions) while the naive decoder flickers.
