    Why the adapter matters: 4× fewer tokens

    The encoder runs at 50 Hz; a 30-minute audio would be 90,000 tokens for the decoder. The adapter's 4-frame concatenation drops that to 12.5 Hz, so the same 32k context window stretches from ~11 minutes to ~43 minutes of audio.
