    Alternating local & global attention

    Gemma 2 interleaves local sliding-window layers (attend only to the last tokens) with global layers (attend to all past tokens). This keeps most layers cheap while a few provide long-range reach. The masks below are the exact tensors used by the demo.

    Local — sliding-window mask

    Query i may attend to keys in (i−window, i]. Note the banded diagonal.

    Global — full causal mask

    Query i may attend to every key j ≤ i: the full lower triangle.

    Logit soft-capping

    Instead of letting scores grow without bound, Gemma 2 squashes them with cap · tanh(x / cap). Small values pass through almost unchanged; large values saturate toward ±cap. The paper uses 50 for attention scores and 30 for the final logits.

    The soft-cap transfer function

    Dashed = identity (no cap). Solid curves = cap·tanh(x/cap) for cap = 30 and 50.

    Grouped-query attention (GQA)

    Many query heads share a smaller set of key/value heads — shrinking the KV cache with little quality loss.
