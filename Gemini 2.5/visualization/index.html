Why it works & the rest of Gemini 2.5

Independent slips cancel

  • Each path errs on a different step, so wrong answers scatter across the vocabulary.
  • The correct answer is the single most reinforced outcome → it wins the vote.
  • More paths ⇒ lower variance ⇒ higher, saturating accuracy.
