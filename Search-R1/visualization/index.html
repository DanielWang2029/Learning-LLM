Search-R1 · RL + Search

Train a policy with reinforcement learning to interleave reasoning with a real search tool: <search> → <information> → … → <answer>. The learned search procedure generalizes to entities it never trained on — memorization does not.

Interleaved reason → search → answer

A real rollout of the trained policy on a held-out question. It follows the 2-hop chain and skips the distractor facts.

The RL loop: group rollouts → group-relative advantage

For one question the policy samples a group of rollouts. Each gets a reward (correct answer minus a small per-search cost). The advantage is reward minus the group mean — efficient correct rollouts are reinforced, wasteful or wrong ones suppressed. No value network.

reward = correct − 0.2·searches advantage>0 ↑ advantage<0 ↓
