wav2vec 2.0

Self-supervised speech representation learning. A CNN encodes the raw waveform into latent frames; some are masked; a Transformer fills them in; and a contrastive loss forces each masked step to identify its own product-quantized code among distractors. Click a stage to see what it does.

Baevski et al., 2020 arXiv:2006.11477 Masked contrastive Gumbel codebook

The pretraining pipeline

Targets are the quantized latents (right); the context network only sees the masked latents (left). Their agreement at masked steps is the training signal.
