Compressed Convolutional Attention

ZAYA1-8B compresses keys and values with a lightweight convolution before attention, so queries attend over a shorter latent sequence — cutting attention compute and the KV-cache while preserving quality.

How CCA works

A strided depthwise convolution downsamples the K/V sequence by a factor r into L/r latent tokens; queries attend over those (paper §II-A-1).
