The efficient-open-model thesis

LLaMA's central claim: trained on enough public data with the right recipe, a much smaller model beats a much larger one. Smaller models are also cheaper to serve.

The LLaMA block, end to end

A stack of identical decoder blocks. Each sub-layer is pre-normalized: the norm is applied to the input, and the residual carries the un-normalized signal.
