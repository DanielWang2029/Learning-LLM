Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback

Align a model to an explicit constitution without human labels: the model critiques its own responses against written principles, revises them to comply, and then a preference model is trained on AI feedback (RLAIF). Reproduced on toy text so it runs on CPU.

Bai et al., 2022 arXiv:2212.08073 Self-critique + revision RLAIF
