    The masked-LM objective

    During pre-training, ~15% of positions are chosen; of those 80% become [MASK], 10% a random token, 10% are left unchanged. The bidirectional context makes the answer recoverable.

    Key equations

    Bidirectional self-attention

    Attention(Q,K,V) = softmax(Q Kᵀ / √d_k) V head_i = Attention(Q Wᵢ^Q, K Wᵢ^K, V Wᵢ^V) MultiHead = Concat(head_1..head_h) W^O no causal mask -> position i sees all positions j

    Masked-LM loss

    for masked set M: L = - Σ_{i∈M} log p(x_i | x_context) p(.) = softmax( GELU(h W1+b1) · Eᵀ ) (E = tied token embedding)
